Hollow Knight Boss Strategies

The following page will focus on specific strategies you can perform while fighting bosses. A lot of these tricks can be used against multiple bosses, even if the setup is a bit different. This page will be divided into specific sections for each strategy, with the bosses as subcategories.

Note: I primarily play in All Bindings to make the game harder. Being in this mode is not required. However, if you're interested in a guide specific to All Bindings, go here.

Double Fireball Hit

With the spell Vengeful Spirit, (aka fireball) you can often hit certain bosses twice, or even 3 times, if lined up properly. This becomes even easier to pull off when using the Shaman Stone charm, as it increases the size and damage of your spells. Here are some examples with explanations for each boss.

Scream Skips

A Scream Skip is a glitch that allows you to have movement during boss cutscenes, where it would typically prevent movement. This is achieved by getting hit during the frame in which the animation starts. It gives you the opportunity to get free hits on the boss. Here are examples of the bosses you are able to do it on.

Dung Defender

For this boss, you'll want to go in already having soul so that you can pop him out of the ground using descending dark. Then use Dream Nail on him to gain more soul, then use descending dark on top of him to automatically scream skip.
