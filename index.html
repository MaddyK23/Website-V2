Hollow Knight Guides

The purpose of the website is to be a fandom page that focuses on speedrunning and boss strategies specifically. A lot of the information found here can be found elsewhere. However, it is mostly scattered across the web and can be hard to find, so the goal is to organize it here and go more in-depth. Though this is more of a proof of concept than anything.

Note that most of this is technical jargon that will sound like complete gibberish to anyone that is not already familiar with the game.
